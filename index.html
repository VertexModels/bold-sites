// FAQ [ 04 / 05 ]

Questions.

How much does a website cost?

A starter landing page is from AED 3,000, a booking site is AED 5,000 and an online shop is AED 7,500+. We confirm the exact price in a quote before we start.

How do payments work?

A 50% deposit is due to start the project. The rest is due before the site goes live.

How long does it take?

Typical timelines depend on scope and on how quickly content and feedback come in. We agree a plan with you at the start.

Is hosting included?

Hosting is part of our monthly care plans: Basic at AED 250/month (hosting + 1 change) or Growth at AED 500/month (up to 4 changes + a monthly report).

Do I need a monthly plan?

It's optional, but it's the easiest way to keep your site hosted and up to date without worrying about it.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes. We can connect a domain you already own, or help you choose and register one.

Do you build Arabic websites?

We build sites ready for Arabic and right-to-left layouts. Tell us which languages you need when you get in touch.

Where do you work?

We work with small businesses in the UAE and Lebanon, mostly remotely over WhatsApp, email and calls.
